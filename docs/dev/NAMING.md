# Naming

The product is called **Headcount** while the owner decides on a new name: "Headcount" and "HeadcountHQ" are already
in use in this space. The name lives in one place, `packages/protocol/src/brand.ts` (`BRAND.name`, `BRAND.tagline`,
`BRAND.description`). The web app, the landing page, the page head (`apps/web/index.html`, filled in by the `brand`
plugin in `apps/web/vite.config.ts`), the Chief's and coworkers' prompts and the worker's own messages read it from
there. A rename is that one line plus the domain and the Google sign-in app name.

## The owner's favourites (checked 6 Oct 2026)

**Floorplan.** Not recommended.

- There is a live, registered FLOORPLAN mark for related software: IMSI Design's home-design program, Reg. 3647545,
  class 9, renewed through 2029.
- floorplan.com belongs to IMSI.
- Search results are full of AI floor-plan generators and real-estate listings, so people would expect an
  architecture tool.

**Biz-bot / BizBot.** Not recommended.

- A US application, BIZ BOT (Serial 99906311, class 42, pending since June 2026), covers AI software that interacts
  with humans. That overlaps this product directly.
- Several small-business chatbot products already use the name.
- "Bot" works against the promise of warm, human-feeling coworkers.

## Shortlist

US mark status comes from Trademarkia's mirror of USPTO records, not USPTO TSDR directly. Domain status is from
WHOIS and DNS lookups on 6 Oct 2026.

| Name          | Why                                                                     | Quick check                                                                                                                                          |
| ------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Officeful** | "An office full of help": warm and plain, and it says what this is.   | No marks found. officeful.ai was unregistered; officeful.com is listed for sale (premium). Have an attorney assess Microsoft's "Office" marks.          |
| Mainfloor     | Keeps the owner's "floor" idea: where the work happens.                 | Only a dead footwear mark found. mainfloor.ai was unregistered; mainfloor.com is registered ("Coming soon").                                          |
| Teamfloor     | Team plus floor, the closest to "Floorplan".                            | No marks found. teamfloor.ai was unregistered; teamfloor.com belongs to a carpet cleaner.                                                             |

The recommendation is **Officeful**, then Mainfloor, then Teamfloor.

Before committing to any name, get a full clearance search from an attorney: US, EU and Madrid filings, classes 9,
35 and 42. Register the domain promptly once chosen.

Two names to avoid:

- "AI Office" alone is not ownable: competitors already use it, including one with a Chief of Staff agent.
- Avoid names built on "Cowork", which Anthropic's Claude Cowork already uses.

## Draft messaging (if Officeful)

- **Tagline:** An office full of help.
- **Hero:** Your office, fully staffed.
- **One sentence:** Officeful gives small-business owners an AI office: a Chief of Staff you talk to, and a team of
  AI coworkers who meet, plan and deliver real work, checking with you before anything goes out.
- **Messages:**
  - One person to talk to.
  - A team, not a chatbot.
  - Real work out the door: documents, spreadsheets, apps, images, short videos and emails.
  - You stay in charge.
